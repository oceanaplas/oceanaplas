## Hi there 👋
# Oceanaplas

🐋 **Saving marine life through technology.**

A conservation tech startup protecting endangered marine species with AI, robotics and ocean sensing.

- 🌍 **Website** → [oceanaplas.org](https://oceanaplas.org)
- 💼 **Careers** → [oceanaplas.org/careers](https://oceanaplas.org/careers)
- ✉️ **Contact** → [info@oceanaplas.org](mailto:info@oceanaplas.org)

## What we work on

| area | what it is |
|---|---|
| Marine conservation | Tracking and monitoring endangered species in their natural habitats |
| Ocean technology | AI and robotics for ocean cleanup and pollution prevention |
| Habitat protection | Data-driven analysis to plan protected areas and safe zones |
| Research | New methods and tools for marine life preservation |

## Open tech we're exploring

| project | what for |
|---|---|
| [movingpandas](https://github.com/movingpandas/movingpandas) | Analysing animal movement tracks |
| [OpenCV](https://github.com/opencv/opencv) | Computer vision for species and debris detection |
| [ArduPilot](https://github.com/ArduPilot/ardupilot) | Autopilot for marine drones and surface vehicles |
| [ROS 2](https://github.com/ros2/ros2) | Software framework for marine robots |
| [OBIS](https://github.com/iobis) | Open ocean biodiversity data |
| [Global Fishing Watch](https://github.com/GlobalFishingWatch) | Open data on fishing activity at sea |
